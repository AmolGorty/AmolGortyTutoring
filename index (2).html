Get to know me

Hi, I’m Amol. I help students turn the SAT from something stressful into something predictable. In every session we work on the exact skills that can move your score, using real practice questions and clear explanations.

My style is straightforward and encouraging. We skip the filler and go over every mistake until it makes sense. I tutor the SAT because I can relate to students taking it right now, and I explain things in a way that actually makes sense to them.
